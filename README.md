# 🃏 Solitaire Game

Welcome to **Solitaire**, a classic single-player card game built with **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**.

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of Solitaire is to move all cards from the **tableau (main playing area)** to the **foundation piles (top slots)**, organized by suit and in ascending order (Ace → King).

---

## 🚀 How to Start
1. **Download or clone** the project files.
2. **Open** the `index.html` file in your web browser (simply **double-click** the file).
3. The game will load automatically — no setup required!

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

### 🃏 Game Layout
- **Stock Pile** – The face-down deck in the top-left corner.  
- **Waste Pile** – Cards flipped from the stock pile for play.  
- **Foundations** – The four upper slots where cards are placed by suit (♠ ♥ ♦ ♣) from Ace to King.  
- **Tableau** – The seven piles of cards where most of the gameplay happens.  

---

### 💡 Basic Rules
1. **Flip Cards from the Stock**  
   - Click the stock pile to draw a new card into the waste pile.  
   - When the stock is empty, click it again to reset the pile.  

2. **Build on the Tableau**  
   - Arrange cards in **descending order** and **alternating colors** (e.g., Red 7 on Black 8).  
   - Only a **King** can be placed on an empty tableau column.  

3. **Move Cards to Foundations**  
   - Build foundations by **suit** in **ascending order** (Ace → 2 → 3 → ... → King).  

4. **Reveal Face-Down Cards**  
   - When a card move reveals a hidden card beneath it, that card automatically flips face-up.  

5. **Winning the Game**  
   - The game is won when all cards are moved to the four foundation piles.  
   - When all Tableau cards are face-up and the Stock-pile is empty, a Solve button appears — click it to auto-complete the game!  

---

## 🧮 Scoring and Timer
- Your **score** and **timer** are displayed at the top.  
- The timer automatically **stops** when the game is completed.
